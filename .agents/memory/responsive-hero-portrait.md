---
name: Responsive hero portrait separation
description: Visual constraint for the portfolio's photo-backed introduction across screen sizes.
---

Keep hero text and the portrait subject visually separate at narrow breakpoints. A background image can sit behind wrapped text even when it appears correctly positioned on desktop.

**Why:** On this portfolio, shifting the portrait alone did not prevent the mobile headline from crossing the face; the text width and image crop had to be adjusted together.

**How to apply:** When changing the hero headline, copy, or portrait composition, inspect both desktop and phone-sized previews and confirm the text does not obscure the face.
